Privacy Policy for Autofy

Effective Date: 11 November 2026

Autofy, formerly known as AutoPartsSL, is operated by Vihan Mapalagama (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the Autofy mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

Sign-in Information

If you sign in with Google, Google provides your name and email address to us through our authentication provider. Some accounts sign in with a one-time password sent to a phone number. We also store a unique account identifier for each account.

Vehicle and Listing Information

  • Vehicle make, model, and year, including vehicles you save in the app
  • Auto part details, photos, and descriptions you upload
  • For sellers: optional proof files you attach to a listing, as a photo or a PDF (for example an invoice or warranty card)
  • For sellers: store details you choose to publish, such as shop name, store address and city, map location, and contact numbers
  • Quotation requests you submit through the app

Published listings and store details are visible to other users of the app.

Proof files are optional and are not shown on the listing page. They may be reviewed by us to verify a listing. They are stored with our file storage provider in the same storage area as listing photos, which is not private: a person who has the exact link to a file may be able to open it without signing in. Please do not upload documents that contain personal or financial information you would not want others to see.

Location Information

If you use features that need your location, such as accident reporting or roadside assistance, we may collect your device location while the feature is actively in use. Location data is not collected in the background.

To show a place name for your location, your device sends the coordinates directly to BigDataCloud. BigDataCloud also receives your device’s IP address, and states that it uses the pairing of coordinates and IP address to improve its own IP geolocation accuracy. When a seller sets a store location on a map, the device sends the coordinates directly to Geoapify to look up an address. Geoapify keeps request information, including the request content, IP address and a timestamp, for operating its service, and states that data from successful requests is generally kept for no longer than 24 hours. These two providers handle that data under their own privacy policies.

Technical Information

When a listing is viewed, we record the view with a random session identifier that is not linked to your account, so that sellers can see how many views their listings received. Google Play may provide us with aggregated crash and performance reports. Our hosting, backend and location-lookup providers may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and request details, needed to run their services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enable listing and discovery of auto parts
  • Let buyers contact sellers, including through quotation requests and the phone and WhatsApp details sellers publish
  • Improve application performance and stability
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party service providers to operate the app, including:

  • Supabase for database, authentication, and file storage
  • Google for Google sign-in
  • BigDataCloud to convert device coordinates into a place name
  • Geoapify to look up an address for a seller’s store location
  • Firebase for web hosting

Supabase and Firebase process data on our behalf. Google, BigDataCloud and Geoapify receive data directly from your device and handle it under their own policies, as described above. We do not sell your personal data. AI Scan features are not active in the current version of the app; we will update this policy before enabling them.

4. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries outside your country of residence where our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

5. Data Retention

We have not set a fixed retention period for most data. We keep personal information while your account exists and as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. What happens to your data when you delete your account is described on our account deletion page.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the app. Sellers must first remove the listings they are able to remove. If your account is linked to records other users depend on, such as quotation requests or quotations, it is closed instead: your sign-in details and profile are erased, and those records are kept in pseudonymised form, linked to an internal account identifier instead of your name or contact details. Business and case records that involve other users are not erased by account deletion. Full details, and how to contact us if you cannot delete in the app, are on the account deletion page:
https://vihanmapa.github.io/autopartssl-privacy-policy/delete-account/

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below. We review each request individually and cannot guarantee that every request can be completed.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission and secure infrastructure. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Autofy is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
