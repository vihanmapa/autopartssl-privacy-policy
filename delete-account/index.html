Delete your Autofy account

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Autofy app (formerly AutoPartsSL, Android app com.autopartssl.app), operated by Vihan Mapalagama, and how to delete only some of your data while keeping your account. It applies to buyers and sellers. See also our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to the Autofy app.
  2. Buyers: open Profile → Delete Account and confirm.
  3. Sellers: open your seller Settings, go to Danger Zone → Delete Account, and type DELETE to confirm.

2. Sellers: remove your listings first

If you have seller listings, you must remove them yourself before your account can be deleted. Until you do, the app refuses the deletion with the message “Remove your seller listings before deleting your account” and nothing is deleted. A listing that a buyer’s quotation request refers to cannot be removed; you do not need to remove it. When you delete your account it is withdrawn instead, as described in section 3.

3. What happens when deletion succeeds

If no other user’s records depend on your account, your sign-in account and profile are permanently deleted, together with your saved vehicles, reviews, notifications and eligible uploaded files.

If your account is linked to records other users depend on – such as quotation requests you sent or received, or quotations exchanged through Autofy – your account is closed instead:

  • Your sign-in details are removed: your password and sessions are deleted, and your email address, phone number and any Google or Apple sign-in link are replaced with irreversible placeholders. You can no longer sign in to the account.
  • Your profile details are erased: name, email, phone number, addresses, saved vehicles, cart, preferences and, for sellers, your store details, logo and banner.
  • Your reviews, notifications and saved quotations that are not linked to a quotation request are deleted.
  • Quotation requests that are still waiting for a quote are cancelled.
  • Sellers: listings that a quotation request refers to are withdrawn from the marketplace, and their description and photos are removed.
  • The linked records are kept, as described in section 5. They point to an anonymous closed-account reference, not to your name or contact details.

In both cases you may register a new account later with the same email address or phone number. It will start empty and will not carry your previous history.

4. When deletion is refused

The app refuses the deletion, and your account stays active with nothing deleted, if any of these apply:

  • You still have seller listings that you can remove (see section 2).
  • Some of your files cannot be removed safely.
  • A previous deletion attempt needs manual review.
  • The account is an administrator account.

When this happens the app tells you which reason applies. Remove what you are able to remove, then try again. If you cannot resolve it yourself, see section 7.

5. What is kept

  • Records that other users depend on are kept when your account is closed: quotation requests and what they contain (for example the part, vehicle and any notes you wrote), quotations sent in reply to a request, the title and price of withdrawn listings, and any other record another user depends on. They are no longer linked to your name, email address or phone number.
  • Sign-in and security logs kept by our authentication provider are managed under that provider’s own retention rules.
  • We have not set a fixed retention period for these records.

6. Delete some of your data without deleting your account

These actions in the app permanently delete the data from your account, at any time:

  • Saved vehicles: open My Garage and remove the vehicle.
  • Saved addresses: open Profile → Saved Addresses and delete the address.
  • Sellers – listings: open My Listings and delete the listing. The listing and its photos are deleted. A listing that a buyer’s quotation request refers to cannot be deleted.

Changing your name or phone number in Profile replaces it with the new value. That is a correction, not a deletion request.

For anything else – for example saved quotations, reviews, notifications, uploaded files or details you cannot remove yourself – email us as described in section 7, with the subject “Delete some of my Autofy data” and a description of the data you want deleted. We will delete what we can and tell you what we cannot delete and why. Records that other users depend on are kept as described in section 5.

7. Cannot sign in, deletion is refused, or you want some data deleted

Email support@autofy.lk (or vihanvevo@gmail.com) with the subject “Delete my Autofy account” or “Delete some of my Autofy data”. Please include:

  • The email address or phone number registered on the account.
  • Whether you are a buyer or a seller.
  • If deletion was refused, the message the app showed.
  • If you want only some data deleted, what that data is.

Also email us if you can still sign in after the app told you your account was closed. Do not send passwords or identity documents. We will check that the request comes from the account holder, review it manually, and tell you what we can and cannot do. We cannot guarantee that every request can be completed, and we do not promise a response time.
