Delete your Autofy account

Last updated: 08 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Autofy app (formerly AutoPartsSL, Android app com.autopartssl.app), operated by Vihan Mapalagama. It applies to buyers and sellers. See also our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to the Autofy app.
  2. Buyers: open Profile → Delete Account and confirm.
  3. Sellers: open your seller Settings, go to Danger Zone → Delete Account, and type DELETE to confirm.

2. Sellers: remove your listings first

If you have seller listings, you must remove them yourself before your account can be deleted. Until you do, the app refuses the deletion with the message “Remove your seller listings before deleting your account” and nothing is deleted.

3. What happens when deletion succeeds

  • When account deletion succeeds, your sign-in account and profile are permanently deleted. Certain account-related personal records and eligible uploaded files may also be removed. Some records may be retained as explained in Section 5.
  • You may register a new account later. It will not carry your previous history.

4. When deletion is refused

The app refuses the deletion, and your account stays active with nothing deleted, if any of these apply:

  • You still have seller listings.
  • Your account has linked business or case records, such as orders, payments, quotations, cases or evidence.
  • Some of your files are still referenced elsewhere or cannot be removed safely.
  • A previous deletion attempt needs manual review.

When this happens the app tells you which reason applies. Remove what you are able to remove, then try again. If you cannot resolve it yourself, see section 6.

5. What is not erased

  • Business and case records that involve other users are not erased by account deletion. This is why an account with such records cannot be deleted automatically.
  • Sign-in and security logs kept by our authentication provider are managed under that provider’s own retention rules.
  • We have not set a fixed retention period for these records.

6. Cannot sign in, or deletion is refused

Email support@autofy.lk (or vihanvevo@gmail.com) with the subject “Delete my Autofy account”. Please include:

  • The email address or phone number registered on the account.
  • Whether you are a buyer or a seller.
  • If deletion was refused, the message the app showed.

Do not send passwords or identity documents. We will check that the request comes from the account holder, review it manually, and tell you what we can and cannot do. We cannot guarantee that every request can be completed, and we do not promise a response time. In particular, we are not currently able to erase business or case records that other accounts depend on.
